Sauki To-do list

Welcome to Sauki, a simple to-do list that helps you capture your tasks, stay organized, and get things done.

Select New list to get started, or View List to pick up where you left off. Need a hand? The About page and the project repository have a full guide to every feature.

Sauki is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as part of my journey to becoming a better developer. Feedback is always welcome.
